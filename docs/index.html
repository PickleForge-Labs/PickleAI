🤖 About PickleAI

PickleAI is a local-first AI assistant designed to give your computer a powerful personal helper while keeping control on your own machine.

🧠 Memory

Remember important information and keep conversations organized.

💻 PC Control

Launch applications, interact with your system, and automate tasks.

🌐 Internet Mode

Detect internet availability and enable online features when connected.

⚡ Local AI

Powered by Ollama and local AI models.

🛠 Technology

Python • PyWebView • SQLite • Ollama • Qwen2.5
