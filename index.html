🤖 What is PickleAI?

PickleAI is a local AI assistant designed to bring powerful AI tools directly to your desktop.

It combines AI chat, memory, automation, computer tools, local models, and system interaction into one personal assistant.

✨ Features

🧠 Memory

Keep conversations and useful information organized.

💻 PC Control

Interact with applications and automate computer tasks.

⚡ Local AI

Run supported AI models locally through Ollama.

🔒 Privacy Focused

Keep your local AI experience under your control.

🛠 Automation

Build multi-step workflows instead of handling every action manually.

🎛️ Customizable

Designed to grow with new tools, models, and integrations.
